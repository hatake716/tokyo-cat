# TOKYO-CAT adapted facial art

Facial geometry, source albedo and normal textures derive from:

- **Bicolor Cat** by **kenchoo**: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bicolor-cat-e623a618ca344a8393d7ba4d63ec23cf
- Original **3d modelling my cat: Fripouille** by **guillaume bolis**: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/3d-modelling-my-cat-fripouille-0ab14bf98e754f8d90fe1bf1c84ca66c
- **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

TOKYO-CAT modifications: extracted head and eyes, subdivided mesh, coordinate and breed adaptations, recolouring, AI-assisted facial albedo refinement, new rig binding, groom, lighting and blink timing. The original body, rig and locomotion animation are not included. No endorsement by either creator is implied.
